1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Social Media Response Pro ("we", "our" or "the App") collects, uses and protects your information when you use our Instagram automated response service.

2. Information We Collect

When you connect your Instagram business account to our app, we collect:

  • Instagram Business Profile Information: Username, profile photo, and basic account details
  • Messages: Direct Messages (DMs) received on your Instagram account

  • Media: Information about your Instagram posts and content

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Automatically respond to direct messages using AI
  • Moderate and respond to comments on your posts

  • Improve our service and user experience

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is securely stored on our servers. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

5. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Your Instagram data is used exclusively to provide our automated response service.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data at any time through our dashboard

  • Request deletion of all your data
  • Export your data in a readable format

7. Data Retention

We retain your data only while your Instagram account is connected to our service. When you disconnect, all your data will be deleted within 30 days.
